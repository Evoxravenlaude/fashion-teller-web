How a commission works

Designers on Fashion Teller design in the same studio you do, so what they deliver is a real design you can open, see in 3D, change and send to a tailor.

  1. 1. BriefSay what it is for, what you like and your budget. It takes two minutes.
  2. 2. OfferThe designer replies with a fee and a delivery time. Nothing is paid until you accept.
  3. 3. DesignYour payment is held by Fashion Teller. The designer delivers a design; two rounds of changes are included.
  4. 4. YoursApprove it and the design lands in your library, credited to them. Then have it made by any verified tailor.
