Speed check

Measures how smoothly this device draws the 3D view, at each of the picture settings the site can step down to. It takes about 15 seconds.

Keep this tab in front while it runs. Close other apps first for a fair reading.

The live try-on

The try-on depends on the camera and on a person being in view, so it is checked in place. This opens it with a readout in the corner: frames a second, and how long finding the body and dressing it take.

Open the try-on with the readout

The readout stays on for this tab. Add ?fps=0 to any address to switch it off.
