Custom clothing should start with an idea, not a sketch

Getting a garment made means explaining it to a tailor, hoping they picture the same thing, and chasing them for updates. Most people with a clear idea never get it made. Fashion Teller lets you design it yourself and see it first, choose a verified tailor on price, quality and delivery, and follow production to your door.

What it does

Puts the design in your hands

Templates, palettes made for each style and fabric, swappable parts and a finger-sketch layer. See it in 3D and on your own photo. Come back every day for a new challenge.

Connects you with verified makers

Compare tailors and manufacturers side by side on price, rating and delivery time, then choose.

Tracks production to delivery

One tracker from concept to doorstep, so you always know where your order is.

Widens the market for tailors

Workshops quote on designs from customers far outside their local area.

Where we are going

The product you can use today, and what comes next.

Now

Design, measure, order, track

Accounts, concepts with full version history, measurement profiles, verified tailor quotes, payment in naira held until delivery, live production tracking.

Next

Later
