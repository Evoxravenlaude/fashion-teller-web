Custom clothing should start with an idea, not a sketch

Getting a garment made means explaining it to a tailor, hoping they picture the same thing, and chasing them for updates. Most people with a clear idea never get it made. Fashion Teller lets you see the design first, choose a verified tailor on price, quality and delivery, and follow production to your door.

What it does

Turns plain language into a design

Describe a garment and see an AI-generated concept. Refine it in your own words until it is right.

Connects you with verified makers

Compare tailors and manufacturers side by side on price, rating and delivery time, then choose.

Tracks production to delivery

One tracker from concept to doorstep, so you always know where your order is.

Widens the market for tailors

Workshops quote on designs from customers far outside their local area.

Where we are going

The studio is the first part. Templates and brand work come next.
