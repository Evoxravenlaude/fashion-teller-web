Account

Your details

Tailors see your name and city on requests. Your email is used to sign in and for order updates.

Emails

Everything also shows under the bell. Choose what should reach your inbox as well.

Sign-in emails, such as a password reset, are always sent.

Measurement profiles

Measure once, reuse on every order. Keep a profile for each person you order for. Use a soft tape, snug but not tight.

Become a designer

Publish your designs under your own name and take commissions. Clients send a brief, you reply with a fee, and you are paid when they approve the design.
