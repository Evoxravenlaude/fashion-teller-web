Phase 1 of 4

Say what you want, then refine it in your own words

No sketches and no fabric codes. Write a sentence and Fashion Teller turns it into a concept you can look at. Each instruction after that makes a new version, so you see the change before anyone cuts fabric.

"Add deeper gold trim." Concept v2.

Phase 2 of 4

Compare verified tailors and choose one

Tailors and manufacturers who can make the design quote a price and a delivery time. Sort by best match, price, speed or rating. Your approval assigns the vendor and starts the order at the quoted total.

Approve design & initiate production.

Phase 3 of 4

Fabric is sourced and the garment is tailored

The tailor works from the concept and every refinement you approved. The tracker moves as your order does, so you never have to chase anyone for an update.

Vendor assigned, then fabric sourcing & tailoring.

Phase 4 of 4

Checked, then delivered to your door

A quality check before dispatch, then doorstep delivery. The same tracker you watched from the first sentence marks it done.

Doorstep quality delivery.

Questions people ask first

No. Describe the garment the way you would to a friend. You can mention fabric, colour or fit if you know them, and refine anything later in plain words.

Every tailor and manufacturer on Fashion Teller is checked before they can quote. Customers see a verified mark, a rating from past orders, a price and a delivery time for each quote.

The tailor quotes it for your specific design. When you approve, the order is created at that quoted total, never at a number typed in on your side.

Approval locks the version you chose so the tailor can start. Make every change you want before approving; each refinement is saved as its own version.

Delivery depends on the tailor you choose. Each quote shows the delivery time to you, so you can pick a workshop that suits your timeline.
